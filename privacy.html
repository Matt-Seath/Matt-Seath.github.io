Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Larry is a personal, self-hosted assistant operated by its owner for their own private use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public. This policy describes how Larry handles data when the owner connects it to their own Google Account.

What data Larry accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's standard sign-in, Larry may access the owner's own:

  • Gmail — to read and help manage the owner's messages,
  • Google Calendar — to read and manage the owner's events, and
  • Google Contacts — to look up and manage the owner's contacts.

Larry only ever accesses the account of the person who authorized it. It does not access anyone else's data.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to provide personal-assistant features to the owner — for example, summarizing or flagging important email, surfacing upcoming calendar events, and answering questions about the owner's own contacts. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to those features.

Google user data & Limited Use

Larry's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the assistant features above, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features (see below), and is not used for advertising.

Sharing

The owner's data is not sold and is not shared with third parties for their own purposes. To generate responses, relevant content may be processed by the AI model providers that power the assistant (for example OpenAI and Anthropic), acting as processors solely to produce Larry's replies. No other sharing occurs.

Storage & security

Larry runs on the owner's own hardware at the owner's home, reachable only over the owner's private network. Credentials and data stay on that hardware; access is restricted to the owner; backups are kept privately and encrypted.

Retention & revocation

Data is processed as needed to provide the features above and is not retained beyond what the assistant and its private backups require. The owner can revoke Larry's access to their Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends all access.

Contact
